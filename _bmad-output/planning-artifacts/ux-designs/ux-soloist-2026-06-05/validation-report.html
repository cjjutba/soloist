C-1 — Guard validated only white-on-accent, not the accent used AS link text

EXPERIENCE.md › Per-Tenant Branding
The accent is applied to links/active states (accent-as-text on Paper) — the inverse of what the guard checked. A pale brand could clear the button check yet render link text at ~2:1.
Resolved: guard now validates a decoupled tenant-accent-text token ≥4.5:1 on background and white, auto-darkening when a pale fill fails as text.
Resolved · critical

C-2 — No 3:1 non-text floor for accent focus rings / active states

EXPERIENCE.md › Accessibility Floor
A pale Tenant accent used as the focus ring could drop below 3:1, making keyboard focus invisible with no "unreadable text" symptom to warn the Freelancer.
Resolved: explicit 3:1 non-text threshold added; guard checks accent-vs-background, and the focus ring falls back to Soloist Ink when it fails.
Resolved · high

H-1 — Branded email accessibility unspecified

EXPERIENCE.md › Per-Tenant Branding
The day-one "wow" arrives by email; logo alt, images-off degradation, dark-mode inversion, and heading structure were undefined.
Resolved: added an email-accessibility clause (logo alt rule, emoji+label survives images-off, pinned inline backgrounds, ≥14px body, semantic headings).
Resolved · high

H-2 — Status tint pairs passed AA with almost no margin

DESIGN.md › Colors
Shipped 4.76 and In Progress 4.67 sat on the line for small pill text.
Resolved: amber darkened #B45309 → #92400E (5.93:1); all three ratios now documented in the spine.
Resolved · high

H-3 — Live-feed aria-live unspecified (politeness, scope, poll branch)

EXPERIENCE.md › Accessibility Floor / State Patterns
"Use aria-live" without polite/assertive, announced string, or the poll-branch story is not implementable.
Resolved: specified aria-live="polite" with a concise summary, no focus move on insert, and the "Load new updates" control as the announced element on the poll branch.
Resolved · high

EXPERIENCE.md › Accessibility Floor
"Thumb-reachable" addressed reach, not size; shadcn icon buttons default to ~36px.
Resolved: ≥44px hit area for Client Portal controls, ≥24px hard floor for Cockpit.
