## What Went Well

1. **Seam discipline paid off — three of four stories were pure reads.** 3.6 deliberately pre-built the `notifications` table (with `type` as a free string + nullable `ship_update_id`) and the `ship/update.published` fan-out. So 4.1 (center), 4.2 (toast), and 4.3 (emails) added **no backend** — they consumed what was already seeded. The momentum loop assembled fast because the plumbing was poured a story early, on purpose.

2. **One shared query, three consumers.** The `['notifications']` TanStack query backs the bell, the center, AND the 4.2 toaster — deduped, no extra transport. 4.2's `NotificationToaster` is *render-null*: it adds a sonner toast off the shared query with zero new backend. Right altitude.

3. **The a11y bar held across every new surface without a rework cycle** — emoji **+ text** status surviving images-off (4.3 email, the center), ≥44px hit areas, `motion-safe`/reduced-motion guards (4.2 toast, 4.4 switch), semantic headings, logo `alt` + tenant-name fallback. Sally's bar was met first-try each time.

4. **The carried `cache()` debt is finally paid.** Request-scoped `cache()` for `getAppSession`/`getTenant`/`getBranding` was an action item from the **Epic 2** retro, deferred again in Epic 3. The Epic-4 prep closed it **before** the notification bell added per-request reads — exactly the right sequencing. (Bonus learning: React 19's `cache()` is a no-op outside a render, so the per-call test mocks still see distinct results — the old "would memoize across tests" deferral was unfounded.)

5. **Two clarifying forks asked *before* building 4.4** (drive-and-confirm): the pref **shape** (boolean on `client_access` vs a prefs table) and the harder pair — what "other events" 4.4 should actually fire ("pure seam," no contrived event) and what the mute governs ("global mute," not per-channel). Settling those up front kept 4.4 tight and dead-code-free.

## What Was Hard — and the Pattern in the Review Catches

Every story's xhigh review earned its keep, and the **signature was consistent**: not logic bugs on the happy path, but **degradation at the edges** — email-client quirks, browser-tab races, and test strength.

- **4.2 — a browser-tab race.** The anti-burst guard via a `resync` boolean alone was fragile (hidden-at-mount + the focus-refetch-vs-interval race). Fix: a race-proof burst **threshold** in `selectToasts` (suppress when >3 fresh).
- **4.3 — an email-client quirk.** The accent bar as a thin spacer `<Section height:4>` **collapses to 0px in Outlook/MSO** (it ignores table `height`) — the brand accent would vanish in a major client. Fix: a `border-top` on the card (honored everywhere; deletes the fragile spacer).
- **4.4 — test strength.** The isolation foreign-write test pinned its `WHERE` to a known id, so it wouldn't catch a future RLS regression that dropped the engagement clause. Fix: a **WHERE-less** RLS proof (an unqualified foreign-scoped write must hit 0 rows).

**Lesson (Winston):** this class of bug — Outlook rendering, a visibility race, a test that's green-but-weak — is precisely what a *high-effort, render-it / reason-about-the-race* review catches and a lighter pass ships. The recurring **double-`FocusHeading` focus-steal** (a risk in 3.7, 4.1, and re-verified clean in 4.4) is now internalized: never nest two FocusHeadings; the empty state is bespoke.
